**RISE** is an AI-powered academic productivity platform designed to help students manage their **subjects, deadlines, study sessions, timetable, learning resources and academic workload**.

### ✨ What RISE Does

- 🧠 Intelligent subject & topic prioritization
- 📅 AI-assisted daily planning
- 🎓 Google Classroom integration
- 🗓️ Google Calendar integration
- 📚 Notes & document processing
- 🤖 AI Tutor
- 🔎 RAG-based learning from uploaded documents
- ⏱️ Study-session tracking
- 📊 Workload analysis
- 📝 Syllabus & exam timetable management
- 🏫 College timetable integration
- 🔐 Google OAuth + JWT authentication
- ⚙️ Modular Django backend

### 🧠 Intelligence Engine

RISE combines deterministic planning logic with AI capabilities:

```text
Exam Proximity
      ↓
Topic Mastery
      ↓
Difficulty
      ↓
Workload
      ↓
Available Study Time
      ↓
Priority Engine
      ↓
Daily Study Plan
```

### 🛠️ Tech Stack

`React` `Vite` `Django` `PostgreSQL` `OpenAI` `RAG` `Google OAuth` `Google Classroom API` `Google Calendar API` `JWT`

# ⚡ SEROS

### Smart Energy & Resource Optimization System

SEROS uses **computer vision + IoT + intelligent control** to optimize energy usage.

The system can monitor an environment through cameras, determine where resources are actually required, and activate only the necessary sections.

### ✨ Core Idea

```text
Camera / CCTV
      ↓
Computer Vision
      ↓
Detect People / Occupancy
      ↓
Determine Required Zone
      ↓
Smart Grid Control
      ↓
Activate Required Resources
      ↓
Save Energy ⚡
```

### 🛠️ Tech Stack

`Python` `YOLO` `OpenCV` `Django` `ESP32` `IoT` `Computer Vision`

🏆 **Hackathon Winner**

# 🧠 ExamPrep AI

### RAG-Based Academic Assistant

An AI learning assistant that answers questions **from the student's uploaded notes and previous-year question papers**.

### 🔄 Pipeline

```text
PDF / DOCX / PPTX / TXT
          ↓
      Document Loader
          ↓
       Chunking
          ↓
   HuggingFace Embeddings
          ↓
       Chroma DB
          ↓
     Relevant Retrieval
          ↓
        Gemini
          ↓
      Final Answer
```

`Python` `LangChain` `Chroma` `HuggingFace` `Gemini` `RAG`
